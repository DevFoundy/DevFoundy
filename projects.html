Maps & travel04

Midpoint

Find a meeting place that makes the journey fairer for everyone.

Search for real cafés, parks, and train stations using two or more starting locations. Suggestions are ranked by actual travel-time fairness. Includes a map-based website, an Expo mobile app, and a supporting routing and venue API. Searches require the backend; a quiet server may take time to wake.
