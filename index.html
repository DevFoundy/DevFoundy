02 / My current projects

What I’m working on.

Four projects exploring personal assistance, music, fitness, and better ways to meet.

JARVIS

A personal assistant built around a customizable workspace for chat, voice, notes, saved conversations, and personal memory. JARVIS can use tools to research information and present visual answers, and it supports interactive panels such as timers, checklists, planners, and calculators. Its backend runs on a Mac, with local storage for conversations and notes, local voice tools, and configurable add-ons.

StageWrite Studio

A browser-based songwriting workspace for turning musical ideas into chord progressions and arrangements. StageWrite lets you choose a key, genre, and mood, hear a progression, then lock, replace, or edit the chords. It includes guitar, bass, piano, and ukulele views, backing tracks, a drum sequencer, a tuner, and a metronome. Projects save locally and can be exported as MIDI, MusicXML, or chord charts. Its chord generation uses music-theory rules.

KineticForm

A workout planning and tracking app designed around the training session, from choosing a workout to reviewing progress. KineticForm supports reusable routines, an exercise library, set, rep, and weight logging, rest timers, workout history, and progress metrics. It also includes workout-plan imports, exports, and backups. Training data stays on the device by default, with optional Google Calendar scheduling for sessions you choose to add.

Midpoint

A meeting-place finder that helps two or more people choose somewhere convenient to meet. Midpoint uses starting locations to find real venues, such as cafés, parks, and train stations, and ranks suggestions by the fairness of actual travel times rather than straight-line distance. The project includes a map-based website, a mobile app, and a supporting API that searches for venues and calculates routes.
