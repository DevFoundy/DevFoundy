I’m Kenta, and DevFoundy is the name I use to bring my work together. It’s a place to introduce myself through the things I build: my own projects, websites for other people, and ideas that connect technology with everyday needs.

Self-directed projects are one part of that story. They give me a space to follow my own ideas and decide what I want to make. Alongside them, I build custom websites for people who need an online home for their business, their work, or a project they want to share.

I also co-founded a tutoring website. That venture is the education side of my background and an important part of my experience as a co-founder. It sits alongside my independent work as another way I’ve been involved in bringing an idea to life.

Today, DevFoundy brings those different parts of my work into one place. You can explore my projects, discuss a custom website, ask about courses and digital resources, or get in touch about a possible collaboration. Behind each of those conversations is the same person: Kenta.

Before we say hello.

Can you build a website for my idea?

Yes, I build custom websites. Send me a short description of your idea, the pages or features you have in mind, and any examples you like. We can discuss the scope and whether it’s a good fit.

How much does a custom website cost?

It depends on the project. Share what you need, your preferred timeline, and your budget range so we can discuss a suitable approach and quote.

Where can I find courses or digital products?
