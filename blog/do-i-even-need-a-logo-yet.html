do i even need a logo yet

A small branding question with a fairly unfinished answer.

By KentaNovember 4, 20251 min read

I like the idea of a proper logo for a project. Makes it feel like a real thing instead of a folder with a name.

But then there are colours, different versions, an icon, and whether it looks okay when it's tiny. Quite a lot of decisions for something that might just need a title at the top of the page.

Maybe plain text is fine for now.

Not saying logos don't matter. I just don't know whether choosing one helps when the rest of the idea is still changing.

Could leave it until there's something to put the logo on.
