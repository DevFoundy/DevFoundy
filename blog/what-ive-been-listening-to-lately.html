What I’ve Been Listening To Lately

A little about the artists and songs I keep coming back to, how my music taste has changed, and why Glass Eyes stands out to me.

By KentaNovember 8, 20265 min read

I don't think I've really talked much about my music taste on here, so I thought I'd write something a little different from my usual posts about programming, technology, and projects.

Music is something I listen to almost every day, whether I'm going to school, walking between classes, sitting in a car, or just relaxing. I wouldn't say I have one specific genre that I stick to, but there are definitely some artists I keep coming back to.

Right now, the main ones are Radiohead, Muse, Linkin Park, Laufey, and Daniel Caesar. I also enjoy smaller indie artists and some songs from the Hamilton soundtrack.

It's a pretty random combination when you put them all together, but I think that's what makes music interesting.

I think my music taste has developed quite a bit over the past few years.

I originally started by listening to smaller indie artists before getting into Laufey. I actually remember discovering her through articles about her rise to fame, which eventually got me interested in her music.

Around last year, I started listening to Radiohead after discovering their music through a short film I watched. I can't really remember every detail about the film, but it ended up introducing me to a band that I still listen to regularly.

This year, I've been getting more into Daniel Caesar, Linkin Park, and Muse.

I wouldn't say I've completely changed my taste in music, though. I still enjoy the artists I used to listen to. I've just gradually added more genres and artists to my playlists.

Most of the time, I don't even actively search for new music. I just let Spotify recommend songs based on whatever I'm listening to. Sometimes it'll play something completely unfamiliar, and if I like it, I'll save it.

It's a pretty simple way of discovering music, but it works for me.

One thing I've noticed is that what I listen to depends quite a lot on what I'm doing.

If I'm relaxing or just chilling, I'll usually listen to something quieter, like Laufey or Daniel Caesar.

But when I'm walking between classes, going to school, or sitting in a car, I tend to prefer alternative rock, especially Radiohead, Muse, and Linkin Park.

I don't really know how to explain why. I just feel like certain songs fit certain situations better than others.

Sometimes I want something calm, and sometimes I want something with more energy.

I don't think there's much more to it than that.

When it comes to what makes me enjoy a song, I think the melody is probably one of the biggest factors.

I also pay quite a lot of attention to the bass and drums.

Even though I play guitar myself, I'm not necessarily someone who prefers extremely complicated guitar parts or impressive solos. I can appreciate them, but technical complexity isn't really what makes a song good to me.

Sometimes a relatively simple arrangement can sound much better than something incredibly complicated.

I also find myself noticing individual instruments when listening to music. I wouldn't say I'm particularly knowledgeable about music theory, but I can usually focus on the different instruments and hear what they're contributing to the song.

There are definitely songs I listen to that I'd love to play on guitar, although some of them are still way beyond what I can currently play.

Hopefully I'll get there eventually.

I thought I'd share five songs that I've been listening to quite a lot. They're not ranked in any particular order.

  • Falling Away with You — Muse
  • Butterflies & Hurricanes — Muse
  • Glass Eyes — Radiohead
  • Two Faced — Linkin Park
  • Sabotage — Laufey

I think these songs show how varied my taste can be.

Some are relatively quiet and emotional, while others are much heavier and more energetic.

But if I had to recommend just one song from this list to someone who hasn't heard any of them before, I'd probably choose Glass Eyes by Radiohead.

What I find interesting about Glass Eyes is how a song with relatively few instruments can still feel incredibly heavy emotionally.

It doesn't need an enormous instrumental arrangement or a complicated guitar solo to make an impact.

And I think Thom Yorke's vocals are a huge part of that.

When I listen to him sing, it doesn't feel like he's trying to demonstrate how technically good he is. Obviously, there's skill involved, but that's not really what stands out to me.

It sounds like he's simply putting his emotions into the song.

I don't necessarily have a personal memory or experience connected to it, and I don't think I need one.

I can still appreciate the meaning and emotional weight of a song without having experienced the same things myself.

That's probably one of my favourite things about music.

Something else I've thought about is how people sometimes associate certain music genres with particular personalities.

For example, someone might assume that because I listen to Radiohead, I'm a very serious or gloomy person.

But honestly, I don't think I'm a particularly serious person at all. If anything, I'm quite the opposite.

I enjoy music that can be emotional, intense, or even depressing, but that doesn't necessarily reflect how I feel or behave in everyday life.

And I don't really think people should be judged based on what they listen to.

Someone who enjoys heavy rock isn't automatically aggressive, and someone who enjoys sad music isn't necessarily unhappy.

Sometimes people just enjoy how a song sounds.

I think it's as simple as that.

Probably, at least a little.

I've already explored quite a few genres, and I'm sure I'll continue discovering new artists and songs.

But I also think my overall taste will stay relatively consistent.

Even though I've moved between indie music, jazz-influenced pop, alternative rock, R&B, and musical theatre, I still enjoy going back to the artists I've listened to for a while.

I don't really feel the need to stick to one genre or constantly search for something different.

If I like a song, I'll listen to it.

And if I don't, I'll probably just skip it.

Anyway, that's a little bit about the music I listen to. Nothing particularly groundbreaking, but I thought it'd be nice to write something that isn't about coding for once.

Maybe I'll share more of my favourite songs in another post.

For now, if you haven't listened to Glass Eyes by Radiohead, I'd recommend giving it a try.
